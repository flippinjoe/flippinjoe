### 🚀 Things I'm building

**📣 [Cheer Studio](https://cheerstudios.pro)** — iOS · macOS · Android · Web
A routine choreography and team-management platform for competitive cheerleading. Design formations on a true-to-scale floor with a rigged 3D athlete model, sync music, manage teams and athletes, and track skills against the scoring rules of every major governing body — all sharing one canonical data model across native apps and the web.
<sub>`SwiftUI` `SceneKit` `Kotlin/Compose` `Next.js` `PayloadCMS` `Blender/glTF`</sub>

**🎚️ [Baton](https://baton.joesapps.net)** — macOS · [source](https://github.com/flippinjoe/Baton)
A native macOS audio-routing app for complex, multi-device setups. Route any app's audio to any output — all-to-one, one-to-many, per-app splits, even individual channels — with per-app, per-connection, and per-channel volume. Built on Core Audio process taps, so there's no driver, no kernel extension, and no reboot.
<sub>`Swift` `SwiftUI` `Core Audio` `Process Taps` `Realtime IO`</sub>

**🗂️ [Inventory OS](https://flippinjoe.gumroad.com/)** — Notion Templates
A family of Notion templates for inventory management and small-business operations — perishable-inventory tracking, asset maintenance, rental systems, cradle-to-grave audit logs, cost tracking, and a restock dashboard. Sold individually or as one interlinked bundle.
<sub>`Notion` `Databases` `Formulas` `Dashboards`</sub>
